## Limits of the study

- The study measured six of the nine tracks of one album. The model track "Cool 7" was not available.
- The stems come from a separation program. A stem has some sound of the other instruments.
- The drum strokes come from rules for the sound of each drum. The check pictures show that the rules find the loud strokes. Soft strokes and tom-toms are less certain.
- The timing spread has the error of the measurement (about 3 to 5 ms).
- The notes of the stem "other" come from a transcription model. Chords count as some note starts at one time: the values are not the speed of single lines.
- Tempos and keys in the sourced tables are frequently values of databases, made by algorithms. The rows say this.
- The research tasks had a limit for web searches. Some sources were not available. Each task gave a list of the items that it could not verify: the rows with the code K have such items.
- The rows with the code K are not checked.
- The composer of the code cannot hear audio. The checks of the sound are measurements and the judgement of the owner.
