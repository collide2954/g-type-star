# G-Type Star

*G-Type Star* is a new work of electronic jazz fusion: one piece of 13 minutes.
Code makes all parts of the work: the style study, the score, the audio, and the video.

The Sun is a star of type G. The form of the work follows the life of such a star.

## Project status

This repository starts from the engine of
[*Night Music for Orchestra*](https://github.com/collide2954/night-music-for-orchestra):
the music language, the performance layer, the audio engine, and the video tools.
The style study, the design, and the music of *G-Type Star* come in the subsequent commits.
